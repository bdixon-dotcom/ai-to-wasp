Frequently asked questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your account settings, and changes are prorated for the rest of your billing cycle.

Is there a setup fee?

No. There are no setup fees on any Wasp plan. You only pay the listed subscription price, and our team will help you get up and running.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards for monthly and annual billing. Enterprise customers can also arrange invoicing and purchase orders.

How does the free trial work?

Your 15-day free trial includes up to 2 seats and 100 items with full access to barcode and QR tracking. No credit card is required to start.
