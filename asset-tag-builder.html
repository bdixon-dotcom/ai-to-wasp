Asset Tags FAQ

What are asset tags used for?

Asset tags identify and track physical items like laptops, tools, equipment and inventory. They're essential for preventing loss, speeding up audits, and keeping asset records accurate across departments.

What asset tag materials are available?

Choose from polyester, tamper-evident vinyl, weatherproof laminate, or anodized aluminum and metal — each suited to different surfaces, from painted equipment to plastic housings to powder-coated metal.

Can I include a serial number or barcode?

Yes — every tag can include a Code 39, Code 128 or QR code, along with a human-readable ID number. Numbers can be sequential, with an optional prefix or suffix, or pre-assigned through a database merge at checkout.

Are these tags suitable for outdoor or harsh environments?

Yes. Our weatherproof and anodized materials are built to resist fading, chemicals, moisture and abrasion, with outdoor durability ratings from 2 up to 20 years depending on the material you choose.

Do asset tags come on rolls or sheets?

Orders under 1,000 tags ship on sheets; larger orders ship on rolls for use with a compatible printer or applicator. Weatherproof laminate materials always ship on sheets, regardless of quantity.

How does this connect to Wasp Asset tracking software?

Every tag you design here is ready to scan straight into Wasp Asset — so the moment your tags arrive, you can start checking equipment in and out, running audits, and tracking history in real time.
